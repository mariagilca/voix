# Terms and conditions

These terms apply to services provided by voix. The clauses are grouped under
headings so each one can be found and cited.

Last reviewed: 3 August 2026.

## Scope

Services are provided under a written proposal or statement of work that defines
scope, timeline, and fees.

## Payment

A 50% deposit is due at contract signing, with the remaining 50% due upon
delivery.

Fees, payment schedule, and invoicing terms are stated in the proposal, and work
begins after the initial payment is received.

Late payments incur a 5% daily fee.

## Delivery

Standard delivery is within two business weeks from receipt of the deposit and
required materials.

Client materials must be provided on time to meet delivery dates.

## Changes and out-of-scope work

Out-of-scope requests require written approval.

One person can add up to five portfolio materials with our help or independently
using the instructions we provide; additional additions are treated as new
scope.

## Defects

Any bugs we introduce are fixed at no charge.

If a defect is caused by client changes, we will propose a fix priced case by
case, capped at 500 EUR.

## Ownership

Upon full payment, the client owns the final deliverables, while voix retains
rights to pre-existing tools, templates, and know-how.

## Liability

Liability is limited to the amount paid for the services.

## Governing law

The agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Moldova, with venue in
the courts of Chisinau; where applicable, European law may also apply.
