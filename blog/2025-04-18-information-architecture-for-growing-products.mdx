Documentation breaks down when product complexity outgrows the original
navigation model.

<!-- truncate -->

Information architecture is how you prevent that breakdown. It defines how
content is grouped, labeled, linked, and maintained over time.

:::tip IA checkpoint
If two teams describe the same feature with different terms, your IA needs a
controlled vocabulary.
:::

A scalable IA usually includes:

1. A product taxonomy shared across docs, support, and release workflows
2. Information types (concept, task, reference, tutorial) with templates
3. Metadata standards for audience, lifecycle, and product area
4. Governance for ownership, review frequency, and deprecation

When IA is treated as architecture instead of cleanup work, documentation stays
coherent as teams, APIs, and products evolve.
